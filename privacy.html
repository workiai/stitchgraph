Privacy Policy

Your photos are turned into patterns on your iPhone and are never uploaded. Stitchgraph has no account, no ads and no tracking.

Effective October 18, 2026

Who publishes Stitchgraph

Stitchgraph is an app by Abdelilah Cherkaoui. You can reach us at cherkaoui.abdo@gmail.com.

Information on your iPhone

Photos

When you pick a photo or take one, Stitchgraph builds the crochet chart on the device. Separating the subject from its background uses Apple's on-device Vision framework. The photo is not sent to us or to any other service.

Blankets and progress

Your saved blankets, the rows you have checked off, the yarn you mark as owned and your default settings are stored only in the app on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple. We never receive your name, email address or payment details.

To check whether Stitchgraph Pro is active on your device, the app uses RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives a random app user identifier and your purchase records, not your identity. We see aggregated sales figures, such as the number of subscribers, that are not linked to you. See the RevenueCat privacy policy.

What we don't collect

No analytics on how you use the app, no advertising, no advertising identifier, no location and no contacts. We do not sell or share any information.

Your choices and rights

Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or erase on our side. You can delete everything the app stores by deleting the app. For any question, email us, or contact the data protection authority where you live.

Children

Stitchgraph is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Updates to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
