Any photo, crocheted.

Stitchgraph turns a picture into a C2C or tapestry blanket pattern, writes out every row and tells you how much yarn to buy.

Questions

Which photos work best?

One clear subject in daylight, filling most of the frame. A busy background is fine: Stitchgraph can lift the subject off it and put it on a plain yarn color.

C2C or tapestry?

Corner-to-corner (C2C) builds the blanket diagonally from small blocks of three double crochets: quick and cozy. Tapestry single crochet works row by row with smaller stitches, for finer detail. You can switch on every blanket.

How is the amount of yarn calculated?

From the number of blocks or stitches of each color and a standard gauge for DK, worsted or bulky yarn, plus 10% for swatching and color changes. Check your own gauge with a small swatch before you buy.

What does Stitchgraph Pro add?

The full zoomable chart, the row tracker, written rows, unlimited blankets, more yarn colors and the printable PDF. Pro is available as a yearly subscription with a free trial, or as a one-time purchase.

How do I restore or cancel my purchase?

To restore, open Settings in Stitchgraph and tap Restore. To cancel a subscription, open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid the next charge.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Everything happens on your iPhone. Read the privacy policy.
